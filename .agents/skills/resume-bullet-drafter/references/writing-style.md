# Resume Workshop — House Style Reference Guide
*Based on Verlyn Klinkenborg’s 'Several Short Sentences About Writing'*

This style reference governs how accomplishment bullets, executive summaries, and career narratives are drafted in Resume Workshop.

---

## Core Philosophy

> **Writing is revision. Composing, revising, editing, and publishing are not separate phases — they are the single, continuous act of creation.**

1. **The Sentence is the Unit of Attention**
   - Each bullet point must be a single, complete, self-sufficient thought.
   - If a sentence requires a breath or mental juggling, break it into two or prune it.

2. **Every Word Must Earn Its Place**
   - Eliminate filler openings:
     - ❌ "Responsible for leading..."
     - ❌ "Helped to build..."
     - ❌ "Successfully managed..."
     - ❌ "Assisted with the implementation of..."
   - ✅ Start directly with the active, specific verb: *"Architected," "Engineered," "Built," "Migrated," "Reduced," "Negotiated."*

3. **Put Emphasis at the End**
   - In English, the point of maximum rhetorical stress is the sentence end.
   - Lead with the action and context; land with the quantified result and impact.
   - ✅ *"Architected distributed tracing pipeline ingesting 2.5M spans/sec across 60 microservices, reducing MTTR by 42%."*

4. **Be Concrete, Not Abstract**
   - Abstract: *"Improved database performance significantly."*
   - Concrete: *"Partitioned 12TB primary PostgreSQL database, cutting p99 query latency from 180ms to 24ms."*

5. **State the Fact; Imply the Competence**
   - Never assert virtues directly (*"Demonstrated exceptional leadership under pressure"*).
   - State the factual truth of what was done (*"Led 14 engineers through zero-downtime multi-region cloud migration during peak traffic"*). The reader deduces leadership without being told.

6. **Kill Clichés and Corporate Jargon**
   - ❌ "Cross-functional synergy" → ✅ "Partnered with product, security, and design teams"
   - ❌ "Passionate self-starter" → (Delete entirely; demonstrate through achievements)
   - ❌ "Moved the needle on KPIs" → ✅ "Increased daily active conversions by 18%"

7. **Clarity is Not Simplification**
   - Use precise technical terms when they are the exact right word (*"Raft consensus," "gRPC with Protocol Buffers," "eBPF network probes"*).
   - Technical specificity signals authentic seniority.

8. **Alternate Points Cover Different Strengths**
   - When drafting, produce 2–3 variants emphasizing distinct dimensions:
     - Variant A: Raw scale & latency metrics (systems depth)
     - Variant B: Developer velocity & organizational adoption (breadth/impact)
     - Variant C: Reliability & operational cost savings (business alignment)
   - The user selects the active bullet; unpicked variants are saved as inactive alternates in the vault.
